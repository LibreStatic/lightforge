Lightforge is a private, offline-first gallery for Android, built for big photo and video libraries. Browse, search, organize, edit and protect your media on your own device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

EARLY ACCESS BETA
Lightforge is in beta. Most features are ready to use, but some are still maturing, and the object eraser and subject cut-out tools are labeled experimental. Your feedback shapes what comes next.

BUILT FOR LARGE LIBRARIES
• A timeline you pinch to zoom from years down to a single day
• Designed for libraries of 100,000+ photos and videos, including removable SD cards
• Works with Android's photo permissions: share everything or only selected items
• No all-files access required

SEARCH THAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
• Search by keyword, date, place, people, pets and text inside photos
• Optional semantic search that understands what a photo shows (requires downloading a model in Settings)
• Every search runs on your device; your photos and queries are never uploaded

PEOPLE, PETS AND PLACES
• Optional, opt-in face grouping with manual corrections, all processed locally
• Pet recognition for cats and dogs
• An offline Places map built from the locations stored in your photos
• Pause or delete all analysis data at any time

PRIVATE ALBUMS AND APP LOCK
• Store photos and videos in an encrypted private album protected by your biometrics
• Lock the whole app behind biometrics or your device credential

PHOTO EDITOR
• Filters, crop, straighten and precise adjustments
• Camera RAW development with highlight recovery and lens correction
• Edits are saved as copies unless you choose to overwrite

VIDEO EDITOR
• Trim, change speed, add music and adjust color
• LOG footage support, LUT import and HDR export on supported devices

PDF STUDIO
• Lay out photos and documents on pages
• Rework imported PDFs and export them locally

MORE TO CREATE AND ORGANIZE
• Albums, favorites, trash and smart collections
• Memories grouped by time and place, turned into short videos
• Collage and GIF makers
• Motion photo playback
• A home screen widget

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR TERMS
• Send originals to another Android device over your local network with an encrypted connection
• Mirror your library to a folder or back it up to your own servers
• Available in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian and German
• Light, dark and dynamic Material You colors, with support for phones, foldables and tablets

PRIVACY
Lightforge does not collect your data. Your photos, videos and analysis results stay on your device. The internet is used only when you ask for it, for example to download a search or pet-recognition model, or to reach your own server or another device on your network.

Lightforge is open source under the Apache License 2.0. The source code is available on GitHub.
