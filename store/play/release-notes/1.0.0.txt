<en-US>
Lightforge Studio 1.0:
• Foldables: Show on outer screen, or Face to face.
• Hold on video and Double tap on video: choose slow motion, fast forward, rewind, zoom or skip for each side.
• Local analysis shows real progress, what is left and an estimate.
• Individual pets are recognized in the background; you confirm the suggested groups.
• Faster start with large libraries.
Report problems at github.com/LibreStatic/lightforge/issues
