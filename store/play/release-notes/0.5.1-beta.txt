<en-US>
• On supported phones, slow motion can run on the DSP, which uses less battery. Choose it in Settings > Video playback > "Frame interpolation"; "Automatic" uses the DSP when available, otherwise the GPU.
• "Save slow-mo clip" no longer fails when the hold ends early in the video.
Report problems at github.com/LibreStatic/lightforge/issues
</en-US>
